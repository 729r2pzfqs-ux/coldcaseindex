Wrongful and Disputed Convictions

Cases in which a conviction was overturned or the convicted person was exonerated, and cases in which a conviction has been publicly disputed. Listing a case here does not mean the conviction was wrong: each entry states precisely what a court has and has not found. ColdCaseIndex lists 23 cases on this theme, of which 5 are recorded as unsolved.
