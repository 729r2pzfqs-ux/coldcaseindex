Cold Cases and Forensic Genetic Genealogy

Cases in which investigators used forensic genetic genealogy: comparing DNA from a crime scene or from unidentified remains against public genealogy databases to build a family tree that points to a name. The list includes victims and suspects identified this way and unidentified-remains cases where the work is still under way. ColdCaseIndex lists 41 cases on this theme, of which 12 are recorded as unsolved.
