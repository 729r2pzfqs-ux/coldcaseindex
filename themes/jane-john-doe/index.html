Jane Doe and John Doe Cases

People who were known for years only by a placeholder name because their remains could not be identified. Some have since been given their names back, usually through DNA; the rest are still waiting. The type hub for unidentified persons lists every case of that type, named or not. ColdCaseIndex lists 19 cases on this theme, of which 12 are recorded as unsolved.
