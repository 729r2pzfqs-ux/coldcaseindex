Civil Rights Era Cold Cases

Killings connected to the civil rights movement and to racial terror in the United States, many of them left unprosecuted for decades. Some were reopened under the Emmett Till Unsolved Civil Rights Crime Act and ended in convictions forty years or more after the crime. ColdCaseIndex lists 21 cases on this theme, of which 4 are recorded as unsolved.
