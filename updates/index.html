Recent Changes to the Index

Every build compares each case with its previous version and records what changed: cases added or removed, status changes, timelines that gained events, and entries revised after a check against their sources. Wording fixes and site-wide template changes are not listed. A status changes only when a cited source reports the development, and people who have not been convicted remain presumed innocent.

Follow the Atom feed for new cases, status changes and timeline updates. See also cases to watch, whose status is expected to change, and on this day.
