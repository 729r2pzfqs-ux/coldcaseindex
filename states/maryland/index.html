Cold Cases in Maryland

ColdCaseIndex documents 9 cases in Maryland, dated 1969 to 2023. 4 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Catherine Cesnik (1969) and the most recent is Rachel Morin (2023).

Conviction 2023

Rachel Morin

Rachel Morin, a 37-year-old mother of five, was raped and murdered on the Ma & Pa Trail in Bel Air, Maryland, on August 5, 2023; her body was found the next day.

Bel Air Homicide Age 37
No Conviction 2015

Freddie Gray

Freddie Gray died of spinal cord injuries sustained while in Baltimore police custody. Six officers were charged but all were acquitted or had charges dropped. His death sparked the 2015 Baltimore uprising.

Baltimore Suspicious Death Age 25
Pending Trial 2014

Sarah and Jacob Hoggle

Two-year-old Sarah and three-year-old Jacob Hoggle were last seen with their mother, Catherine Hoggle, in Montgomery County, Maryland, in September 2014 and have never been found.

Clarksburg Missing Person
Unsolved 2006

Rey Rivera

Rey Rivera, a 32-year-old writer and videographer, vanished from his Baltimore home in May 2006 after a phone call and was found dead a week later beneath a hole in the roof of the historic Belvedere Hotel.

Baltimore Homicide Age 32
Conviction 1999

Hae Min Lee

High school student Hae Min Lee was strangled and her body found in Leakin Park, Baltimore, in 1999. Her ex-boyfriend Adnan Syed was convicted of first-degree murder in 2000, and his case became the subject of the podcast Serial.

Baltimore Homicide Age 18
Unsolved 1981

Sebastian Russo

Dr. Sebastian Russo, 56, a Baltimore family physician known for charging patients $5 or less, was shot dead in his Harford Road office on the evening of February 27, 1981.

Baltimore Homicide Age 56
Wanted 1976

Bradford Bishop Family

Annette Bishop, 37, her mother-in-law Lobelia, 68, and sons William, 14, Brenton, 10, and Geoffrey, 5, were beaten to death at their Bethesda home on March 1, 1976.

Bethesda Multiple Homicide
Unsolved 1976

Margaret Fetterolf (Woodlawn Jane Doe)

On September 12, 1976, the body of a teenage girl who had been beaten, sexually assaulted, and strangled was found near Lorraine Park Cemetery in Woodlawn, Baltimore County, Maryland.

Woodlawn Unidentified Person Age 16
Unsolved 1969

Catherine Cesnik

Sister Catherine Cesnik, a 26-year-old Baltimore nun and teacher, vanished in November 1969 and was found bludgeoned to death months later.
