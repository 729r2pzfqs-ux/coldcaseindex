Cold Cases in Texas

ColdCaseIndex documents 40 cases in Texas, dated 1884 to 2024. 14 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is The Servant Girl Annihilator (Austin Axe Murders) (1884) and the most recent is Audrii Cunningham (2024).
