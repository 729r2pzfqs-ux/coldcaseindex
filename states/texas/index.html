Cold Cases in Texas

ColdCaseIndex documents 48 cases in Texas, dated 1885 to 2024. 21 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is The Servant Girl Annihilator (Austin Axe Murders) (1885) and the most recent is Audrii Cunningham (2024).
