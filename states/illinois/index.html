Cold Cases in Illinois

ColdCaseIndex documents 30 cases in Illinois, dated 1911 to 2025. 18 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Elsie Paroubek (1911) and the most recent is Chicago River North Mass Shooting Victims (2025).
