Cold Cases in New Jersey

ColdCaseIndex documents 18 cases in New Jersey, dated 1922 to 2019. 9 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Hall–Mills Murders (1922) and the most recent is Dulce Maria Alavez (2019).

Unsolved 2019

Dulce Maria Alavez

Five-year-old Dulce Maria Alavez disappeared from a playground at Bridgeton City Park while her mother sat in a nearby car. A witness reported seeing a man lead the child away.

Bridgeton Missing Person Age 5
Conviction 2016

Sarah Stern

Sarah Stern, 19, was strangled at her Neptune City, New Jersey, home on December 2, 2016, during a robbery, and her body was thrown from the Route 35 bridge; it has never been recovered.

Neptune City Homicide Age 19
Ruled Suicide 2015

Tiffany Valiante

Tiffany Valiante, an 18-year-old athlete from Mays Landing, New Jersey, was struck and killed by a New Jersey Transit train in Galloway Township in July 2015 after walking away from her home.

Galloway Township Homicide Age 18
Unsolved 2006

The Eastbound Strangler (Atlantic City Four)

Four women, all involved in sex work and struggling with addiction, were found strangled and posed face-down in a drainage ditch behind the Golden Key Motel outside Atlantic City in November 2006.

Egg Harbor Township / Atlantic City Multiple Homicide
Unsolved 2002

John Gilbride

John Gilbride, 34, a US Airways baggage supervisor, was shot in his car outside his apartment complex in Maple Shade, New Jersey, and found at 12:08 a.m. on September 27, 2002.

Maple Shade Homicide Age 34
Unsolved 1996

Susan Walsh

Freelance journalist Susan Walsh disappeared from her Nutley, New Jersey apartment while investigating the Russian mafia's involvement in strip clubs. She had expressed fear for her life. She was never found.

Nutley Missing Person Age 36
Conviction 1994

Megan Kanka

Seven-year-old Megan Kanka was sexually assaulted and murdered on July 29, 1994, by Jesse Timmendequas, a neighbor in Hamilton Township, New Jersey, who had two prior convictions for sex offenses against children.

Hamilton Township Homicide Age 7
Unsolved 1991

Mark Himebaugh

Eleven-year-old Mark Himebaugh disappeared on November 25, 1991, near his home on Sunray Road in Del Haven, Middle Township, New Jersey, after going to watch firefighters tackle a nearby brush fire.

Del Haven Missing Person Age 11
No Conviction 1991

Timothy Wiltsey

Five-year-old Timothy Wiltsey vanished from a carnival in Sayreville, New Jersey, in May 1991, and his skeletal remains were found nearly a year later.

Sayreville Homicide Age 5
Unsolved 1990

Susan Negersmith

Susan Negersmith, a 20-year-old from Carmel, New York, was found dead in Wildwood, New Jersey, over Memorial Day weekend 1990. Her death was first ruled accidental and later reclassified as a homicide.

Wildwood Homicide Age 20
Arrest Made 1982

Dawn Olanick (Princess Doe)

The body of a teenage girl, beaten beyond recognition, was found beside Cedar Ridge Cemetery in Blairstown Township, New Jersey, on July 15, 1982.

Blairstown Unidentified Person Age 17
Unsolved 1975

David Williams & Steven Anderson

David Williams, 12, and his roommate Steven Anderson, 17, disappeared on April 7, 1975, from the New Lisbon State School, a residential center for people with developmental disabilities in Burlington County, New Jersey.

Pemberton Township Missing Person
Unsolved 1974

Margaret Ellen Fox

Margaret Ellen Fox, 14, disappeared on June 24, 1974, after taking a bus from Burlington, New Jersey, to Mount Holly to meet a man who had answered her babysitting advertisement and called himself John Marshall.

Burlington Missing Person Age 14
Conviction 1971

List Family Murders

John List shot his wife, his mother and his three teenage children at their home in Westfield, New Jersey, on November 9, 1971, and disappeared. The bodies were found on December 7, 1971.

Westfield Multiple Homicide
No Conviction 1968

Jane Durrua

Jane Durrua, 13, of Keansburg, New Jersey, disappeared on November 4, 1968, and was found dead in a field in Middletown the next morning.

Keansburg Homicide Age 13
Unsolved 1962

William 'Billy' Jones Jr.

Three-year-old William "Billy" Jones Jr. disappeared on December 17, 1962, while playing outside with his younger sister in Vineland, New Jersey. He was last seen near a neighbor's house at about 11:45 a.m.

Vineland Missing Person Age 3
Conviction 1932

Charles Lindbergh Jr.

The infant son of aviator Charles Lindbergh was kidnapped from the family estate in Hopewell, New Jersey. A ransom was paid but the baby's body was found two months later.

Hopewell Homicide Age 1
No Conviction 1922

Hall–Mills Murders

Episcopal priest Edward Wheeler Hall and choir singer Eleanor Mills, who were having an affair, were found shot to death in a field near New Brunswick, New Jersey, in September 1922.
