Cold Cases in Missouri

ColdCaseIndex documents 20 cases in Missouri, dated 1950 to 2024. 11 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Janet Christman (1950) and the most recent is Gypsy Rose Blanchard (release) (2024).
