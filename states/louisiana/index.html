Cold Cases in Louisiana

ColdCaseIndex documents 8 cases in Louisiana, dated 1912 to 2022. 3 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Bobby Dunbar (1912) and the most recent is Lacey Fletcher (2022).

Conviction 2022

Lacey Fletcher

Lacey Fletcher, 36, was found dead on January 3, 2022 on a couch in her parents' home in Slaughter, Louisiana, after years of severe neglect.

Jeff Davis 8 Victims

Eight women and girls were found dead in and around Jefferson Davis Parish between 2005 and 2009. Many were involved in prostitution or drug use.

Derrick Todd Lee Victims

Derrick Todd Lee was the Baton Rouge serial killer who murdered at least seven women between 1992 and 2003. DNA evidence finally linked the crimes and led to his arrest.
