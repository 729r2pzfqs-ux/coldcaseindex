Cold Cases in Australia

ColdCaseIndex documents 15 cases in Australia, dated 1948 to 2021. 6 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Somerton Man (1948) and the most recent is Cleo Smith (2021).

Regions covered: New South Wales (3), South Australia (2), Victoria (1), Queensland (1), Northern Territory (1).

Conviction 2021

Cleo Smith

Four-year-old Cleo Smith was taken from her family's tent at the Blowholes campsite north of Carnarvon, Western Australia, on October 16, 2021. Police found her alive 18 days later, on November 3, in a locked house in Carnarvon.

Unsolved 2014

William Tyrrell

Three-year-old William Tyrrell vanished while playing in his foster grandmother's front yard in Kendall, New South Wales, wearing a Spider-Man costume.

Kendall Missing Person Age 3
Solved 2012

Jill Meagher

Irish-born ABC radio journalist Jill Meagher was raped and murdered while walking home in Brunswick, Melbourne. Adrian Bayley, a serial rapist on parole, was sentenced to life.

Melbourne Homicide Age 29
Conviction 2003

Daniel Morcombe

Thirteen-year-old Daniel Morcombe disappeared while waiting for a bus at Woombye on Queensland's Sunshine Coast on December 7, 2003.

Woombye Abduction Age 13
Conviction 2001

Peter Falconio

British tourist Peter Falconio was shot on the Stuart Highway near Barrow Creek in Australia's Northern Territory on July 14, 2001; his girlfriend Joanne Lees escaped.

Barrow Creek Homicide Age 28
Unsolved 1997

Marion Barter

Australian schoolteacher Marion Barter secretly changed her name before vanishing during an overseas trip. She legally became 'Florabella Natalia Marion Remakel' before disappearing.

Southport Missing Person Age 51
Conviction 1996

Claremont Serial Murders

Three women vanished from the upscale suburb of Claremont in Perth, Australia between 1996-1997. Telstra technician Bradley Edwards was convicted of two murders in 2020.

Perth Serial Killer Victims
Conviction 1989

Ivan Milat (Backpacker Murders)

Seven backpackers aged 19 to 22, from Australia, Germany and Britain, disappeared while hitchhiking from Sydney between December 1989 and April 1992; their bodies were found in the Belanglo State Forest in 1992 and 1993.

Belanglo State Forest Serial Killer Victims
Solved 1988

Scott Johnson

American mathematician Scott Johnson was found dead at the base of cliffs in Manly, Sydney. Initially ruled a suicide, his death was reclassified as a gay hate crime. Scott White pleaded guilty to manslaughter in 2023.

Sydney Homicide Age 27
Unsolved 1987

Mr. Cruel Victims

Between 1987 and 1991, an unidentified offender known as 'Mr Cruel' attacked an 11-year-old girl in her Lower Plenty home and abducted and sexually assaulted two girls in suburban Melbourne, releasing both.

Conviction 1982

Lynette Dawson

Lynette Dawson vanished from her Sydney home. Her husband Chris Dawson, a former rugby league player and teacher, was convicted of her murder in 2022 after the podcast 'The Teacher's Pet' revived interest.

Two fifteen-year-old girls were stabbed to death at Wanda Beach in Sydney's south on a hot summer day. Despite one of Australia's largest investigations, the killer was never identified.
