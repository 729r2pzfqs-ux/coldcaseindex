Saronno Hospital Patient Deaths (Leonardo Cazzaniga case)

Leonardo Cazzaniga, a former deputy head of the emergency department at Saronno Hospital in Lombardy, Italy, was convicted of killing patients with overdoses of morphine, anaesthetics and sedatives.
