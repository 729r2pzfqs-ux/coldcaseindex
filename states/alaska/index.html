Cold Cases in Alaska

ColdCaseIndex documents 6 cases in Alaska, dated 1971 to 2020. 2 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Robert Hansen Victims (1971) and the most recent is Florence Okpealuk (2020).

Unsolved 2020

Florence Okpealuk

Florence Okpealuk, a 33-year-old Inupiaq mother originally from Wales, Alaska, was last seen on August 31, 2020, leaving a tent on West Beach about one to two miles outside Nome.

Nome Missing Person Age 33
Partially Solved 2012

Israel Keyes Victims

Israel Keyes confessed to killing Samantha Koenig, 18, abducted in Anchorage on February 1, 2012, and Bill and Lorraine Currier, who vanished from Essex, Vermont, in June 2011.

Anchorage Serial Killer Victims
Unsolved 1995

Erin Marie Gilbert

Erin Marie Gilbert, a 24-year-old Anchorage nanny, vanished from the Girdwood Forest Fair in Girdwood, Alaska, on July 1, 1995, while on a first date.

Girdwood Missing Person Age 24
Conviction 1993

Sophie Sergie

Sophie Sergie, 20, of Pitkas Point, Alaska, was found sexually assaulted, stabbed and shot in a dormitory bathroom at the University of Alaska Fairbanks on April 26, 1993.

Fairbanks Homicide Age 20
Partially Solved 1980

Eklutna Annie

Eklutna Annie is the name given to an unidentified young woman whose body was found on July 17, 1980, in a shallow grave near Eklutna Lake Road outside Anchorage, Alaska.

Anchorage Unidentified Person
Conviction 1971

Robert Hansen Victims

Robert Hansen was an Anchorage baker who abducted women, flew them to remote Alaska wilderness, and hunted them. He confessed to 17 murders and was convicted in 1984. He died in prison in 2014.
