Cold Cases in Massachusetts

ColdCaseIndex documents 24 cases in Massachusetts, dated 1892 to 2023. 12 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Andrew & Abby Borden (1892) and the most recent is Ana Walshe (2023).

Ana Walshe

Ana Walshe, 39, was last seen at her Cohasset, Massachusetts home in the early hours of January 1, 2023; her body has never been found.

Cohasset Homicide Age 39
No Conviction 2022

John O'Keefe (Karen Read)

Boston police officer John O'Keefe, 46, was found dead in the snow outside a home in Canton, Massachusetts, on January 29, 2022.

Canton Homicide Age 46
Pending Trial 2021

Sandra Birchmore

Sandra Birchmore, 23, was found dead in her Canton, Massachusetts, apartment on February 4, 2021, and the state medical examiner ruled her death a suicide by hanging.

Canton Homicide Age 23
Conviction 2013

Amy Lord

Twenty-four-year-old Amy Lord was kidnapped from her South Boston apartment, forced to withdraw money from ATMs, then murdered. Edwin Alemany was convicted and sentenced to life.

Boston Homicide Age 24
Conviction 2006

Rachel & Lillian Entwistle

Rachel Entwistle, 27, and her nine-month-old daughter Lillian Rose were shot dead in their rented home in Hopkinton, Massachusetts, on January 20, 2006; police found them two days later.

Hopkinton Homicide
Unsolved 2000

Molly Bish

Molly Bish, 16, vanished on June 27, 2000, minutes after her mother dropped her off for her lifeguard shift at Comins Pond in Warren, Massachusetts. Her belongings were left at her post.

Warren Homicide Age 16
Unsolved 1996

Karina Holmer

Karina Holmer was a 20-year-old Swedish au pair whose dismembered body was found in a Boston dumpster in June 1996, roughly a day after she was last seen leaving a nightclub. The grisly killing has never been solved.

Boston Homicide Age 20
Unsolved 1995

Deanna Cremin

Seventeen-year-old Deanna Cremin was found strangled behind a senior housing complex on Jaques Street in Somerville, Massachusetts, on the morning of March 30, 1995, a short distance from her home.

Somerville Homicide Age 17
Unsolved 1993

Holly Piirainen

Ten-year-old Holly Piirainen disappeared on August 5, 1993, after leaving her grandparents' home in Sturbridge, Massachusetts, to see a neighbor's puppies. Hunters found her remains in Brimfield on October 23, 1993.

Sturbridge Homicide Age 10
Conviction 1992

Lisa Ziegert

Lisa Ziegert, 24, who worked at a middle school, was abducted from her evening job at a card shop in Agawam, Massachusetts, on April 15, 1992, and found dead four days later.

Agawam Homicide Age 24
Solved 1989

Carol Stuart

Carol Stuart, 30 and pregnant, was shot in a car in Boston's Mission Hill on October 23, 1989 and died the next morning; her son, delivered by caesarean, lived 17 days. Her husband Charles blamed a Black gunman.

Boston Homicide Age 30
Unsolved 1988

New Bedford Highway Killer Victims

Between March 1988 and April 1989, eleven women with ties to New Bedford, Massachusetts, vanished; the bodies of nine were found dumped along Route 140, Interstate 195, and nearby roads, while two women were never found.

Mary Jo Kopechne, 28, died when a car driven by Senator Edward Kennedy went off Dike Bridge into Poucha Pond on Chappaquiddick Island late on July 18, 1969. Her death was attributed to drowning.

Partially Solved 1962

Boston Strangler Victims

Thirteen women were strangled in the Boston area between 1962 and 1964. Albert DeSalvo confessed to all 13 murders but was never tried for them. DNA evidence collected in 2013 linked DeSalvo to the last victim, Mary Sullivan.

Boston Serial Killer Victims
Unsolved 1961

Joan Risch

Joan Risch vanished from her Lincoln, Massachusetts home, leaving behind a trail of blood and signs of a violent struggle. She had recently checked out several library books about disappearances. She has never been found.

Lincoln Missing Person Age 31

Andrew Borden and his wife Abby were found hacked to death in their Fall River, Massachusetts home. Their daughter Lizzie was acquitted of the murders.
