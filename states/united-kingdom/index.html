Cold Cases in United Kingdom

ColdCaseIndex documents 35 cases in United Kingdom, dated 1888 to 2023. 15 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Jack the Ripper Victims (1888) and the most recent is Brianna Ghey (2023).

Regions covered: London (6), Scotland (1), North Yorkshire (1), South Yorkshire (1), Wales (1), Suffolk (1).
