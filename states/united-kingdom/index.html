Brianna Ghey

Sixteen-year-old transgender girl Brianna Ghey was murdered in a park in Warrington by two 15-year-olds. The killers had planned the attack for weeks. Both received life sentences.

Constance Marten & Mark Gordon

Constance Marten and Mark Gordon went on the run across England after their newborn baby died. The baby's body was found in a bag in Brighton. Both were convicted of manslaughter by gross negligence.

Brighton Homicide

David Carrick Victims

Metropolitan Police officer David Carrick pleaded guilty to 49 offenses including 24 rapes against 12 women over 17 years. He used his status as a police officer to prey on victims.

Neonatal nurse Lucy Letby was convicted of murdering seven babies and attempting to murder six others at the Countess of Chester Hospital. She received a whole-life order.

Nicola Bulley

Nicola Bulley vanished while walking her dog by the River Wyre in Lancashire, England. Her body was found in the river three weeks later. The case drew massive attention and amateur sleuthing.

Sarah Everard (Wayne Couzens)

Metropolitan Police officer Wayne Couzens kidnapped, raped, and murdered Sarah Everard while she walked home in London. He used his police credentials to make a false arrest. Sentenced to whole life.

London Homicide Age 33

Former Russian double agent Sergei Skripal and his daughter Yulia were poisoned with Novichok nerve agent in Salisbury, England. Both survived. Russian GRU officers were identified as suspects.

Two-year-old Liam Fee was murdered by his mother Rachel and her partner Nyomi Fee in Glenrothes, Scotland. He had suffered prolonged abuse. They also abused two other children.

Joanna Dennehy Victims

Joanna Dennehy murdered three men and stabbed two others in Peterborough and Hereford. She is one of only three women in the UK to receive a whole-life order.

Peterborough Serial Killer Victims

Five-year-old April Jones was abducted and murdered in Machynlleth, Wales by Mark Bridger. Only small bone fragments were ever recovered. Bridger was sentenced to whole life.

Four-year-old Daniel Pelka was starved and beaten to death by his mother and stepfather in Coventry, England. He had been so hungry he stole food from bins at school. Multiple agencies failed to protect him.

Former Russian spy Alexander Litvinenko was poisoned with polonium-210 in London. A British inquiry concluded Russian President Putin probably approved the operation. Two suspects were identified.

Serial killer Peter Tobin murdered at least three young women. Bodies of two early victims were found under his former home 17 years after they disappeared. Tobin died in prison in 2022.

Holly Wells & Jessica Chapman

Ten-year-olds Holly Wells and Jessica Chapman were murdered by school caretaker Ian Huntley in Soham, Cambridgeshire. The case led to major reforms in criminal record checks for people working with children.

Fifteen-year-old Dena Dean was found stabbed to death in a park in Tamworth, England on her way to school. Despite extensive investigation, her killer has never been identified.

Fred and Rose West murdered at least 12 young women at their home at 25 Cromwell Street, Gloucester. Victims were buried in the garden and cellar. Fred hanged himself before trial; Rose was convicted.

Antiques dealer Trevaline Evans vanished from her shop in Llangollen, Wales, leaving a 'Back in 2 minutes' sign on the door. She was never seen again.

Estate agent Suzy Lamplugh vanished after leaving to meet a client named 'Mr. Kipper' in London. Serial killer John Cannan is the prime suspect but has never been charged. Suzy was declared dead in 1994.
