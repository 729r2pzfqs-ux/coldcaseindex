Cold Cases in South Carolina

ColdCaseIndex documents 15 cases in South Carolina, dated 1944 to 2024. 6 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Betty June Binnicker & Mary Emma Thames (1944) and the most recent is Zelig Williams (2024).
