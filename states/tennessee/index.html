Cold Cases in Tennessee

ColdCaseIndex documents 17 cases in Tennessee, dated 1969 to 2024. 8 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Dennis Lloyd Martin (1969) and the most recent is Anthony "Big Jook" Mims (2024).
