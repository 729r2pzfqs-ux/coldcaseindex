Cold Cases in Washington

ColdCaseIndex documents 20 cases in Washington, dated 1947 to 2019. 9 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is William Davidson & Frank Brown (1947) and the most recent is Nikki Kuhnhausen (2019).

Nikki Kuhnhausen

Seventeen-year-old transgender girl Nikki Kuhnhausen was killed in June 2019 by David Bogdanov after he learned she was transgender. Her remains were found on Larch Mountain in Clark County six months later.
