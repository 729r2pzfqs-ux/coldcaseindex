Cold Cases in New Hampshire

ColdCaseIndex documents 9 cases in New Hampshire, dated 1971 to 2021. 4 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Katherine Ann Alston (Bedford Jane Doe) (1971) and the most recent is Elijah Lewis (2021).

Elijah Lewis

Five-year-old Elijah Lewis of Merrimack, New Hampshire, was reported missing on October 14, 2021, and his remains were found nine days later in woods in Abington, Massachusetts.

Merrimack Homicide Age 5
Pending Trial 2019

Harmony Montgomery

Five-year-old Harmony Montgomery is believed to have died in Manchester, New Hampshire, in December 2019 but was not reported missing until late 2021; her remains have not been found.

Manchester Homicide Age 5
Conviction 2013

Abigail Hernandez

Fourteen-year-old Abby Hernandez disappeared while walking home from school in Conway, New Hampshire. She reappeared nearly nine months later, having been held captive by Nathaniel Kibby. Kibby was sentenced to 45 years in prison.

Conway Missing Person Age 14
Unsolved 2004

Maura Murray

UMass Amherst student Maura Murray crashed her car on a remote road in New Hampshire and vanished before help arrived. A witness offered assistance but she declined.

Haverhill Missing Person Age 21
Conviction 1990

Pamela Smart (case)

Gregg Smart, 24, was shot dead in his Derry, New Hampshire, condominium on May 1, 1990. The gunman, 15-year-old William Flynn, was a student at the school where Smart's wife Pamela worked.
