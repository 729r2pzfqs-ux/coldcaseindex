Unsolved 1998

Tristan Brübach

Thirteen-year-old Tristan Brübach was brutally murdered in an underground pedestrian passage in Frankfurt, Germany. The case remains one of Germany's most notorious unsolved child murders.

Unsolved 1981

Katrice Lee

Two-year-old British toddler Katrice Lee vanished from a NAAFI shop on a British military base in Paderborn, Germany, on her second birthday. She has never been found.

Unsolved 1922

Hinterkaifeck Farm Victims

Six people were murdered with a mattock at the Hinterkaifeck farmstead in Bavaria, Germany. The killer apparently stayed at the farm for several days after the murders, feeding the livestock.
