Serenity June Dennard

Nine-year-old Serenity Dennard ran away from the Black Hills Children's Home near Rockerville, South Dakota, on the frigid morning of February 3, 2019, and was last seen walking north on Rockerville Road without a coat.

Unsolved 2013

Rachel Lucille Cyriacks

Rachel Cyriacks, a 30-year-old mother of three from Woonsocket, South Dakota, vanished on November 13, 2013, after driving to Huron to pick up her estranged husband, Brad Cyriacks, who was being released from jail.

Anna Mae Aquash

Anna Mae Pictou Aquash, a 30-year-old Mi'kmaq activist in the American Indian Movement, was shot in mid-December 1975; her body was found near Wanblee on the Pine Ridge Reservation on February 24, 1976.
