Cold Cases in Pennsylvania

ColdCaseIndex documents 19 cases in Pennsylvania, dated 1949 to 2012. 12 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Dorothy Forstein (1949) and the most recent is Kortne Stouffer (2012).
