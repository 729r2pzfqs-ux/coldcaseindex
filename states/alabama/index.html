Cold Cases in Alabama

ColdCaseIndex documents 10 cases in Alabama, dated 1944 to 2019. 3 are recorded as unsolved. The earliest is Recy Taylor (1944) and the most recent is Aniah Blanchard (2019).

Aniah Blanchard

Nineteen-year-old Aniah Blanchard, stepdaughter of UFC fighter Walt Harris, disappeared from Auburn, Alabama, in October 2019. Her remains were found a month later in Macon County.

Amore Wiggins ("Opelika Jane Doe")

Skeletal remains of a young girl found at an Opelika, Alabama, mobile home park on January 28, 2012, went unidentified for almost 11 years. In January 2023 police named her as Amore Joveah Wiggins, born in 2006.

Brittney Wood

Brittney Nicole Wood, 19, was last seen on May 30, 2012, leaving west Mobile, Alabama, with her uncle Donnie Holland, who was found shot in his SUV two days later in a death ruled a suicide.

Natalee Holloway

Natalee Holloway, 18, of Alabama, disappeared in Aruba on May 30, 2005, after leaving a nightclub with Joran van der Sloot and two other men. Her remains have never been found. In October 2023 van der Sloot pleaded guilty in U.S.

Frank Hilley

Frank Hilley died on May 25, 1975, in a death first attributed to infectious hepatitis. After his daughter Carol was found to have been poisoned with arsenic in 1979, his body was exhumed and arsenic was detected.

Conviction 1965

Jimmie Lee Jackson

Jimmie Lee Jackson, 26, a church deacon and voting rights activist, was shot by Alabama state trooper James Bonard Fowler in Marion on February 18, 1965, and died eight days later.

Conviction 1963

16th Street Baptist Church Bombing Victims

A dynamite bomb planted by Ku Klux Klan members killed Addie Mae Collins, Carole Robertson and Cynthia Wesley, all 14, and Denise McNair, 11, at Birmingham's 16th Street Baptist Church on September 15, 1963.

Birmingham Multiple Homicide
Unsolved 1959

Daniel Barter

Four-year-old Daniel "Danny" Barter vanished on June 18, 1959, from his family's campsite on Perdido Bay, Alabama. A search lasting more than a week, with hundreds of volunteers, divers and bloodhounds, found no trace of him.

Perdido Bay Missing Person Age 4
No Conviction 1944

Recy Taylor

Recy Taylor, a 24-year-old Black sharecropper, was abducted and raped by six white men while walking home from church in Abbeville, Alabama, on September 3, 1944. Two all-white grand juries declined to indict anyone.
