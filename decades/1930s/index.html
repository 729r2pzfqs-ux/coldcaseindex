Unsolved 1937

Amelia Earhart

Pioneer aviator Amelia Earhart and navigator Fred Noonan vanished over the Pacific Ocean during her attempt to circumnavigate the globe. She was declared legally dead in 1939.

California Missing Person Age 39
Unsolved 1935

Cleveland Torso Murders

At least 12 people were killed, decapitated and dismembered in and around Cleveland's Kingsbury Run between September 1935 and August 1938. Only two victims were positively identified and no one was ever convicted.

Unsolved 1935

Thelma Todd

Film actress Thelma Todd, 29, was found dead of carbon monoxide poisoning in her car inside a Pacific Palisades garage on December 16, 1935.

California Suspicious Death Age 29
Conviction 1932

Charles Lindbergh Jr.

The infant son of aviator Charles Lindbergh was kidnapped from the family estate in Hopewell, New Jersey. A ransom was paid but the baby's body was found two months later.

New Jersey Homicide Age 1
Ruled Suicide 1932

Paul Bern

MGM producer Paul Bern, 42, was found dead of a gunshot wound at his Beverly Hills home on September 5, 1932, two months after marrying actress Jean Harlow. A note was found and a coroner's jury ruled the death a suicide.

California Suspicious Death Age 42
Unsolved 1931

Starr Faithfull

Starr Faithfull, 25, was found dead on the beach at Long Beach, New York, on June 8, 1931, three days after leaving her family's Manhattan apartment. An autopsy found she had drowned and had bruises and a sedative in her system.

New York Suspicious Death Age 25
Unsolved 1930

Judge Joseph Force Crater

New York Supreme Court Justice Joseph Force Crater, 41, vanished on the evening of August 6, 1930, after dining with two companions at a restaurant on West 45th Street in Manhattan.

New York Missing Person Age 41
Unsolved 1930

Mary Agnes Moroney

Two-year-old Mary Agnes Moroney was taken from her family's Chicago home on May 15, 1930 by a woman calling herself Julia Otis, who never returned her.
