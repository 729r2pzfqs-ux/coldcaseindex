Daniel Barter

Four-year-old Daniel Barter disappeared from his family home near Perdido Bay, Alabama on June 18, 1959. Despite searches, he was never found. The FBI listed his case as one needing fresh leads.

Nine Russian hikers died under mysterious circumstances in the northern Ural Mountains. They had cut their way out of their tent in the middle of the night and fled into subzero temperatures. Some had severe injuries.
