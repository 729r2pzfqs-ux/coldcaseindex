Unsolved 1959

Daniel Barter

Four-year-old Daniel "Danny" Barter vanished on June 18, 1959, from his family's campsite on Perdido Bay, Alabama. A search lasting more than a week, with hundreds of volunteers, divers and bloodhounds, found no trace of him.

George Reeves

George Reeves, the actor who played Superman on television, died of a gunshot wound to the head at his Benedict Canyon home in Los Angeles on June 16, 1959, at age 45.

Unsolved 1957

Joseph Augustus Zarelli

A young boy's battered, naked body was found in a cardboard box in Fox Chase, Philadelphia. Known for decades as 'America's Unknown Child,' he was finally identified in 2022 as Joseph Augustus Zarelli through DNA genealogy.

Pennsylvania Homicide Age 4

Barbara & Patricia Grimes

Sisters Barbara, 15, and Patricia Grimes, 12, vanished on December 28, 1956, after going to see an Elvis Presley film at Chicago's Brighton Theater.

Steven Damman

Two-year-old Steven Damman was taken from his stroller outside a Long Island bakery on Halloween 1955. His mother had left him briefly with his baby sister. Despite FBI involvement and national attention, Steven was never found.

Unsolved 1954

Marilyn Sheppard

Marilyn Sheppard was bludgeoned to death in her Bay Village, Ohio home. Her husband, Dr. Sam Sheppard, was convicted of murder in 1954 but his conviction was overturned. Retried in 1966, he was acquitted.

Conviction 1953

Bobby Greenlease

Six-year-old Bobby Greenlease was taken from his Kansas City school on September 28, 1953, by Bonnie Heady and killed the same day by Carl Austin Hall. His family paid a $600,000 ransom before his body was found.

Unsolved 1953

Evelyn Hartley

Fifteen-year-old babysitter Evelyn Hartley was abducted from a La Crosse, Wisconsin home while watching a professor's daughter. Blood, a shoe, and her broken glasses were found at the scene.

Unsolved 1953

Frank Olson

Army biochemist Frank Olson, 43, died on November 28, 1953, after falling from a window of the Hotel Statler in New York, nine days after CIA colleagues gave him LSD without his knowledge. The death was treated as a suicide.
