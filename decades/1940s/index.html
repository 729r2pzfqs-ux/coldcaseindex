Dorothy Forstein vanished from her locked Philadelphia home while her children slept upstairs. She had previously survived a brutal beating by an unknown attacker.

Texarkana Moonlight Murders

Five people were killed and three wounded in four nighttime attacks in and around Texarkana, on the Texas-Arkansas border, between February 22 and May 3, 1946. The attacker, called the Phantom Killer, was never identified.

Unsolved 1945

Sodder Children

Five of the ten Sodder children disappeared during a Christmas Eve house fire in Fayetteville, West Virginia. No remains were found in the ashes.

Unsolved 1944

Betty June Binnicker & Mary Emma Thames

Betty June Binnicker, 11, and Mary Emma Thames, 8, were found killed in Alcolu, South Carolina, on March 23, 1944. George Stinney Jr., a 14-year-old Black boy, was convicted in a one-day trial and executed on June 16, 1944.

No Conviction 1944

Recy Taylor

Recy Taylor, a 24-year-old Black sharecropper, was abducted and raped by six white men while walking home from church in Abbeville, Alabama, on September 3, 1944. Two all-white grand juries declined to indict anyone.
