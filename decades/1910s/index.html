Unsolved 1918

New Orleans Axeman Victims

An unidentified attacker known as the Axeman struck homes in and around New Orleans between May 1918 and October 1919, killing six people and injuring several others, many of them Italian grocers and their families.

Elsie Paroubek

Elsie Paroubek, a Chicago girl of four or five, disappeared on April 8, 1911, while walking to her aunt's house. A month later her body was recovered from the drainage canal near Lockport, about 35 miles away.
