Unsolved 1928

Glen & Bessie Hyde

Newlyweds Glen and Bessie Hyde disappeared while attempting to run the Colorado River through the Grand Canyon on a homemade scow. Their boat was found intact but the couple was never seen again.

Unsolved 1925

Percy Fawcett Expedition

British explorer Colonel Percy Fawcett, his son Jack, and friend Raleigh Rimmell disappeared in the Brazilian Amazon while searching for a lost city Fawcett called 'Z.' Despite over 100 rescue and investigative expeditions…

Bobby Franks

Fourteen-year-old Bobby Franks was abducted and killed in Chicago on May 21, 1924, by Nathan Leopold and Richard Loeb, two wealthy students.

No Conviction 1922

Hall–Mills Murders

Episcopal priest Edward Wheeler Hall and choir singer Eleanor Mills, who were having an affair, were found shot to death in a field near New Brunswick, New Jersey, in September 1922.

New Jersey Multiple Homicide
Unsolved 1922

Hinterkaifeck Farm Victims

Six people, including two children, were beaten to death with a mattock at the isolated Hinterkaifeck farmstead near Waidhofen, Bavaria, on the night of March 31, 1922.

Germany Multiple Homicide
Unsolved 1922

William Desmond Taylor

Film director William Desmond Taylor, 49, was shot in the back at his bungalow in the Alvarado Court Apartments in Los Angeles on the evening of February 1, 1922; his body was found the next morning.

No Conviction 1921

Virginia Rappe

Actress Virginia Rappe died in San Francisco on September 9, 1921, of peritonitis from a ruptured bladder, four days after falling ill at a Labor Day party in Roscoe "Fatty" Arbuckle's suite at the St. Francis Hotel.
