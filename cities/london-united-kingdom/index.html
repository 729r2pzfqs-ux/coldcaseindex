Sarah Everard, 33, was kidnapped while walking home in Clapham, south London, on March 3, 2021, by serving Metropolitan Police officer Wayne Couzens, who used his warrant card and handcuffs to detain her.

Stephen Port killed four young men with overdoses of GHB at his flat in Barking, east London, between June 2014 and September 2015, after meeting them online.

Former Russian spy Alexander Litvinenko was poisoned with polonium-210 in London. A British inquiry concluded Russian President Putin probably approved the operation. Two suspects were identified.
