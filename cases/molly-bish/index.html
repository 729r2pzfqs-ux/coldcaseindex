Molly Bish

Molly Bish, 16, vanished on June 27, 2000, minutes after her mother dropped her off for her lifeguard shift at Comins Pond in Warren, Massachusetts. Her belongings were left at her post. Her remains were found on June 9, 2003, in woods in nearby Palmer. No one has been charged; in 2021 the Worcester County district attorney named a man who died in 2016 as a person of interest.

Case background and investigation

Molly Anne Bish was 16 and working a summer job as a lifeguard at Comins Pond in Warren, Massachusetts, when she disappeared on the morning of June 27, 2000. She was born on August 2, 1983, the daughter of Magi and John Bish.

According to a CBS News account of the day, Magi Bish drove her daughter to the pond at about 10 a.m. and left around 10:20 after watching her walk toward the beach. At roughly 10:30 a woman arriving at the pond found a first-aid kit, a backpack, a towel, sandals and a water bottle at the lifeguard station, but no lifeguard. A town parks official alerted police about an hour later. Officers first considered that she might have left with friends or drowned before the case was treated as an abduction, and the Massachusetts State Police took over the investigation.

Magi Bish has said that on the previous morning she saw a man near the beach whose presence struck her as suspicious, and a composite sketch was circulated during the investigation. The search that followed has been described as the most extensive and expensive in the state's history.

In the fall of 2002 a hunter came across a blue swimsuit in the woods on Whiskey Hill in Palmer, about five miles from the Bish home. On June 9, 2003, searchers recovered Molly Bish's remains in the area. The condition of the remains did not allow a cause of death to be determined, but authorities have treated the case as a homicide.

Several men have been examined over the years, and none has been charged. In 2021, Worcester County District Attorney Joseph Early publicly identified Francis "Frank" Sumner Sr. as a person of interest, saying investigators had received information they considered credible. Sumner had been convicted in 1982 of aggravated rape and kidnapping and died in 2016. Boston.com reported that he resembled the man Magi Bish had described. He was never charged in the case and the allegation has not been tested in court.

In a June 2023 update, Early said investigators had identified a significant number of evidence items that could be re-examined using newer forensic methods and that the tip line remained active. Bish's parents founded the Molly Bish Foundation in 2004 to promote child safety. The case remains unsolved, and the district attorney's office continues to ask for information at 508-453-7575.
