Suzy Lamplugh (suspect)

Estate agent Suzy Lamplugh vanished after leaving to meet a client named 'Mr. Kipper' in London in 1986; she was declared dead in absentia and her body has never been found. Police named convicted murderer and rapist John Cannan as the prime suspect in 2002, but he was never charged, always denied involvement, and died in prison in November 2024. The case remains unsolved.

Case background and investigation

On July 28, 1986, Suzy Lamplugh, a 25-year-old estate agent, left her office in Fulham, London to show a property to a client she had recorded as 'Mr. Kipper.' She was never seen again.

Suzy's car was found parked about a mile from the property with her purse inside. There were no signs of a struggle. She was declared dead in 1994, presumed murdered.

John Cannan, a convicted rapist released from a prison hostel three days before Suzy's disappearance and later convicted of the 1987 murder of Shirley Banks, was named by police as the prime suspect in 2002; he was never charged, denied involvement, and died in prison on November 6, 2024. He had used the alias 'Kipper' and matched witness descriptions. Police searched properties linked to Cannan in 2018.

Despite extensive investigation, no body has been found and no charges have been brought. The Suzy Lamplugh Trust, established by Suzy's mother, became a leading personal safety charity in the UK.
