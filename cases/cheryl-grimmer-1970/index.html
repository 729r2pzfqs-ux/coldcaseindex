Unsolved January 12, 1970 Missing Person

Cheryl Grimmer

Three-year-old Cheryl Grimmer was taken from outside the shower block at Fairy Meadow Beach, Wollongong, on January 12, 1970. A man was charged in 2017, but prosecutors dropped the case in 2019 after his 1971 confession, made as a teenager, was ruled inadmissible. In 2025 the case was included in a NSW parliamentary inquiry and in 2026 the DPP announced a review.

Case background and investigation

On January 12, 1970, the Grimmer family, recent British immigrants to Australia, visited Fairy Meadow Beach near Wollongong, New South Wales. Three-year-old Cheryl went into a shower and changing block with her three older brothers. When the boys came out, Cheryl was gone.

A witness reportedly saw a man carrying a small child from the area, but the description was vague. Despite an immediate search and widespread publicity, Cheryl was never found.

The case lay dormant for decades until renewed investigation in the 2010s. In 2017, a man was arrested and charged with Cheryl's murder based on a witness statement from the 1970s. However, the charges were dismissed when the court ruled that the suspect's interview as a teenager in 1971 was inadmissible.

Cheryl Grimmer's disappearance remains one of Australia's most enduring cold cases. Her family, including her brothers who were present at the time, have continued to seek answers.
