Case Background & Investigation

On May 4, 2001, Bonny Lee Bakley was shot and killed while sitting in a car outside Vitello's restaurant in Studio City, Los Angeles. Her husband, actor Robert Blake, said he had returned to the restaurant to retrieve a gun he'd left behind.

Blake was arrested and charged with murder. His trial drew comparisons to the O.J. Simpson case—another celebrity accused of killing a spouse.

Blake was acquitted of murder in 2005 but found liable for Bakley's death in a civil suit and ordered to pay $15 million. He was unable to pay.

Blake died on March 9, 2023 at age 89. The murder of Bonny Lee Bakley officially remains unsolved.
