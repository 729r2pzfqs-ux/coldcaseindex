FSU Chi Omega Murders

Status Conviction
Type Multiple Homicide
Date January 15, 1978
Location Tallahassee, Florida
Victim Age Unknown

Ted Bundy broke into the Chi Omega sorority at Florida State University, killing two women and injuring two others. He also attacked a student at another location that night. Bundy was identified via bite mark evidence and convicted in 1979. His execution in 1989 ended his killing spree.
