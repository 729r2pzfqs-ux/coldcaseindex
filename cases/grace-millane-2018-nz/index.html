Grace Millane

British backpacker Grace Millane was murdered in Auckland, New Zealand on the eve of her 22nd birthday by Jesse Kempson, a man she met on Tinder. He was convicted of murder in November 2019 and sentenced to life with a minimum of 17 years.

Case background and investigation

On December 1, 2018, British backpacker Grace Millane went on a Tinder date in Auckland, New Zealand, the evening before her 22nd birthday. She never returned to her hostel.

Her date, Jesse Kempson, then 26, whose name was suppressed by court order until December 2020, took her to his apartment at the CityLife Hotel. He killed her during the night, put her body in a suitcase, and buried it in the Waitakere Ranges outside Auckland.

The killer was identified through CCTV footage showing him with Grace and purchasing a suitcase. He claimed Grace died accidentally during consensual rough sex.

He was convicted of murder in November 2019 and sentenced to life imprisonment with a minimum period of 17 years. The case prompted discussions about safety in online dating and the 'rough sex defense.'
