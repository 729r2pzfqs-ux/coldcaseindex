Reeva Steenkamp

Reeva Steenkamp, a 29-year-old model and law graduate, was shot dead through a locked toilet door at the Pretoria home of her boyfriend, Paralympic sprinter Oscar Pistorius, on February 14, 2013. He was first convicted of culpable homicide, but on appeal he was convicted of murder and his sentence was raised to 13 years and 5 months. He was paroled in January 2024.

Case background and investigation

Reeva Steenkamp, 29, was a model and law graduate who had been in a relationship with South African Paralympic sprinter Oscar Pistorius for about three months, according to CBS News and Wikipedia. In the early hours of February 14, 2013, Pistorius fired four shots through the locked door of a toilet cubicle at his home in Pretoria and killed her, according to France 24 and ABC News (Australia).

Pistorius said he had mistaken Steenkamp for an intruder. Prosecutors argued that the couple had argued late that night and that he shot her knowingly, NBC News reported. The televised trial in Pretoria drew worldwide attention.

In September 2014 the trial judge acquitted Pistorius of murder and convicted him of culpable homicide. In October 2014 he was sentenced to five years in prison, according to France 24 and CBS News. He was later released to house arrest while the prosecution appealed. On December 3, 2015, the Supreme Court of Appeal overturned the culpable homicide verdict and convicted him of murder, according to Wikipedia and France 24.

In July 2016 the trial court sentenced him to six years for murder, although prosecutors had argued for at least 15. The state appealed again, and in November 2017 the Supreme Court of Appeal increased the sentence to 13 years and 5 months, France 24 reported. Wikipedia reports that the Constitutional Court declined to hear his appeal in 2018.

South Africa's Department of Correctional Services confirmed that Pistorius became a parolee on January 5, 2024, after nearly nine years in custody, according to CBS News and Africanews. He was released from the Atteridgeville Correctional Centre near Pretoria. His parole conditions include anger management and gender-based violence programs, and his parole runs until his sentence expires in December 2029. Reeva's mother, June Steenkamp, said in a statement that there could never be justice when a loved one does not come back.
