Nicola Bulley

Nicola Bulley vanished while walking her dog by the River Wyre in Lancashire, England. Her body was found in the river three weeks later. The case drew massive attention and amateur sleuthing.

Case background and investigation

On January 27, 2023, Nicola Bulley, 45, a mortgage adviser and mother of two, vanished while walking her dog along the River Wyre in St Michael's on Wyre, Lancashire. Her phone was found on a bench, still connected to a work call.

The case generated enormous media coverage and attracted 'armchair detectives' who traveled to the area, filmed residents, and promoted conspiracy theories about her disappearance.

Lancashire Police controversially disclosed that Bulley had struggled with alcohol and the menopause, drawing criticism. The Information Commissioner took no enforcement action and the IOPC found no misconduct, but a November 2023 College of Policing review criticised the disclosure and the force's media handling.

Nicola's body was found in the River Wyre on February 19, about a mile from where she disappeared. The coroner ruled her death was accidental—she had fallen into the river. The case prompted debate about social media speculation and police transparency.
