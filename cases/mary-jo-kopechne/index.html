Mary Jo Kopechne

Mary Jo Kopechne drowned when a car driven by Senator Ted Kennedy went off a bridge on Chappaquiddick Island. Kennedy survived and waited nine hours before reporting the accident. He pleaded guilty to leaving the scene. Many questions about what happened that night remain unanswered.
