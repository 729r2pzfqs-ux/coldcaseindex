Peter Tobin Victims

Serial killer Peter Tobin murdered at least three young women. Bodies of two early victims were found under his former home 17 years after they disappeared. Tobin died in prison in 2022.

Case background and investigation

Peter Tobin was convicted of three murders, but police believe he may have killed many more. His known victims are Angelika Kluk (23, killed 2006), Vicky Hamilton (15, killed 1991), and Dinah McNicol (18, killed 1991).

Tobin murdered Polish student Angelika Kluk in September 2006 at a Glasgow church where he was working as a handyman. Her body was found under the floor.

After his conviction for Kluk's murder, police investigated Tobin's previous homes. Under the garden of his former house in Margate, Kent, they found the remains of Vicky Hamilton and Dinah McNicol—both missing since 1991.

Tobin was convicted of all three murders in separate trials, receiving life with a minimum of 21 years for Kluk, a minimum of 30 years for Hamilton, and a whole-life order for McNicol. He died on October 8, 2022. Operation Anagram investigated whether he was responsible for additional unsolved murders.
