Elijah Vue

Three-year-old Elijah Vue was reported missing from Two Rivers, Wisconsin, on February 20, 2024, and his remains were found in a wooded area in September 2024. His mother, Katrina Baur, and her boyfriend, Jesse Vang, have been charged in connection with his death, including child abuse causing death and hiding a corpse in Vang's case. Both have pleaded not guilty and a joint trial is scheduled for April 2027.

Case background and investigation

On February 20, 2024, three-year-old Elijah Vue was reported missing from an apartment in Two Rivers, Wisconsin. His mother's boyfriend Jesse Vang, 38, was the last person known to have seen the boy.

Vang told police he put Elijah down for a nap and the child was gone when he checked later. Prosecutors allege that Elijah had been subjected to abusive punishments before he disappeared; both defendants deny the charges.

Extensive searches of the Two Rivers area, including the Twin River and Lake Michigan shoreline, have not located Elijah. Search dogs indicated human remains near the river.

Vang was charged with felony physical abuse of a child and bail jumping. Elijah's remains were found on private wooded property in September 2024. Vang and Baur were subsequently charged in connection with his death; both have pleaded not guilty, and a joint trial is scheduled for April 14, 2027.
