Nicole Brown Simpson and Ron Goldman were stabbed to death outside her Brentwood condominium. Nicole's ex-husband O.J. Simpson was tried for the murders in the 'Trial of the Century' and acquitted in 1995. He was later found liable in civil court. The criminal case was never solved.
