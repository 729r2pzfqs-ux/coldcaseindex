Case background and investigation

On June 8, 2023, a man attacked people with a knife at the Paquier gardens playground in Annecy, a lakeside town in the French Alps. He stabbed four toddlers in strollers and two adults.

The children, aged between 22 and 36 months, suffered serious injuries including one who was stabbed in the chest. Bystanders, including a Catholic pilgrim named Henri, tackled the attacker.

The suspect was identified as Abdalmasih H., a 31-year-old Syrian refugee who had been granted asylum in Sweden. French psychiatric experts later found his judgment was impaired but that he remains criminally responsible.

All six victims survived. The attack shocked France and was described by President Macron as 'absolute cowardice.' The attacker was charged with attempted murder. Investigating judges ordered him to stand trial before the Haute-Savoie assize court in June 2026, and on October 6, 2026, the investigation chamber of the Chambéry Court of Appeal upheld that referral, finding that his judgment had not been abolished at the time of the attack. A further appeal to the Cour de cassation remains possible, and no trial date has been set.
