Flora Fire Victims

Four sisters aged 5 to 11 died in an arson fire at their home in Flora, Indiana, on November 21, 2016. Their mother escaped with injuries. No one has been arrested and the Indiana State Police investigation remains open.

Case background and investigation

In the early morning of November 21, 2016, a fire engulfed a home on East Columbia Street in Flora, Indiana. Four sisters—Keyana Davis (11), Keyara Phillips (9), Kerriele McDonald (7), and Kionnie Welch (5)—died in the blaze.

Fire investigators determined the fire was intentionally set. The girls' mother, Gaylin Rose, was injured escaping the fire, as were two police officers.

No one has been arrested. The Indiana State Police continue to investigate, with FBI assistance reported in 2025, and a reward is offered.

The case devastated the small community of Flora and became one of the most significant arson murders in Indiana history.
