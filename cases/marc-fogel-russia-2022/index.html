Marc Fogel

American teacher Marc Fogel was arrested at a Moscow airport in August 2021 with about 17 grams of medical marijuana and sentenced in June 2022 to 14 years in a Russian penal colony. After a long campaign by his family he was designated wrongfully detained by the U.S. government and was released on February 11, 2025, in an exchange for Russian national Alexander Vinnik.

Case background and investigation

Marc Fogel, 61, an American teacher at the Anglo-American School of Moscow, was arrested at a Russian airport on August 14, 2021 for carrying 17 grams of medical marijuana prescribed for chronic pain.

Fogel was convicted and sentenced to 14 years in a Russian penal colony—far exceeding the typical sentence for the amount involved. The harsh sentence was widely seen as politically motivated.

Unlike Brittney Griner, who was detained under similar circumstances, Fogel was not classified as 'wrongfully detained' by the State Department, which would have elevated his case. His family and supporters have lobbied extensively for reclassification.

Fogel was not included in the Griner exchange or the August 2024 multi-country swap. He was later designated wrongfully detained and was released on February 11, 2025, returning to the United States after three and a half years in Russian custody.
