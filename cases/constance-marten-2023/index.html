Constance Marten & Mark Gordon

Constance Marten and Mark Gordon went on the run across England after their newborn baby died. The baby's body was found in a bag in Brighton. Both were convicted of manslaughter by gross negligence.

Case background and investigation

In January 2023, aristocrat Constance Marten and her partner Mark Gordon went on the run across England. Marten had given birth in the back of a car and the couple avoided hospitals and authorities.

Their car was found abandoned and on fire on the M61 motorway. A nationwide manhunt followed as police grew increasingly concerned for the welfare of the newborn.

On February 27, 2023, Marten and Gordon were arrested in the Hollingdean area of Brighton. They did not have the baby. On March 1, police found the remains of baby Victoria in a plastic bag inside a shed on a nearby allotment.

At a first trial in 2024 both were convicted of concealing the birth of a child and perverting the course of justice. At a retrial they were both convicted of manslaughter by gross negligence on July 14, 2025, and on September 15, 2025, each was sentenced to 14 years in prison. The cause of the baby's death could not be ascertained.
