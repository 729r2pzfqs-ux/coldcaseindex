Case background and investigation

Peter Nygard, a Finnish-Canadian fashion mogul worth hundreds of millions, was arrested in Winnipeg, Manitoba on December 14, 2020 on federal charges in the United States, including racketeering and sex trafficking.

Allegations spanned decades. Nygard was accused of using his wealth and fashion empire to lure young women—some underage—to his properties in the Bahamas and elsewhere, where they were drugged and sexually assaulted.

Nygard faced charges in both the U.S. and Canada. At his Toronto trial he was convicted on November 12, 2023 of four counts of sexual assault, and on September 9, 2024 he was sentenced to 11 years in prison. Further proceedings in Canada and the United States were still pending.

The case was often compared to Jeffrey Epstein's, with Nygard similarly using wealth, power, and a network of employees to facilitate the abuse of vulnerable women over many years.
