Russell Williams Victims

Colonel Russell Williams, commander of Canada's largest airbase, was convicted of murdering two women after years of escalating crimes. His interrogation by Detective Sergeant Jim Smyth is studied worldwide.

Case background and investigation

Colonel Russell Williams was the commanding officer of CFB Trenton, Canada's largest airbase. He was secretly a serial criminal who escalated from break-ins and lingerie theft to sexual assault and murder.

Williams murdered Corporal Marie-France Comeau, 37, in November 2009, and Jessica Lloyd, 27, in January 2010. He also sexually assaulted two other women during home invasions.

Williams was identified through tire tracks near Jessica Lloyd's home that matched his SUV. In a legendary interrogation by Detective Sergeant Jim Smyth, Williams broke down and confessed within hours, providing detailed accounts and evidence.

Williams was sentenced to two life terms with no parole eligibility for 25 years. He was stripped of his military rank and his medals were burned.
