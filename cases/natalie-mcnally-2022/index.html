Case background and investigation

On December 18, 2022, Natalie McNally, 32, was stabbed to death in her home in Lurgan, County Armagh, Northern Ireland. She was 15 weeks pregnant.

Natalie's family campaigned publicly for justice. She had worked as a civil servant and was described as a loving sister and daughter.

Stephen McCullagh was arrested and charged with murder. Evidence included DNA, phone data, and CCTV footage placing him near Natalie's home.

McCullagh was convicted of murder on March 23, 2026, after a trial that began in February 2026, and on June 3, 2026, was sentenced to life with a minimum term of 31 years. The case highlighted violence against women and the particular vulnerability of pregnant women to domestic violence.
