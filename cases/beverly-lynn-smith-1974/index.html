Unsolved December 9, 1974 Homicide

Beverly Lynn Smith

Twenty-two-year-old Beverly Lynn Smith was shot in the back of the head in the kitchen of her home in Raglan, north of Oshawa, Ontario, on December 9, 1974, while her infant daughter was in the next room. A neighbour, Alan Smith (no relation), was charged in 2008 and again in 2009, but the first charge was dropped and he was acquitted in July 2014. No one has been convicted and the case remains unsolved.

Case background and investigation

On the evening of December 9, 1974, 22-year-old Beverly Lynn Smith was at the home she shared with her husband Doug and their 10-month-old daughter in Raglan, a hamlet north of Oshawa, Ontario. Doug was away from the house and, when Beverly did not answer his phone calls, asked a neighbour to check on her. The neighbour's wife found Beverly dead in the kitchen, killed by a .22-calibre bullet to the back of the head; the couple's infant daughter was unharmed in the next room.

There were no signs of forced entry, and nothing appeared to have been stolen. Beverly's purse and valuables were untouched. The scene suggested she had opened the door to someone she knew. A neighbor reported hearing what might have been a gunshot but did not investigate.

The Durham Regional Police investigation was extensive but hampered by limited forensic technology of the era. Over the decades, the case was reviewed multiple times. In 2022, the case was the subject of the Amazon Prime Video documentary series 'The Unsolved Murder of Beverly Lynn Smith,' which brought renewed attention to the investigation and to the failed prosecution.

A neighbour, Alan Smith (no relation), was charged with the murder in March 2008, but the charge was dropped; he was charged again in December 2009 after an undercover police operation and was acquitted in July 2014. No one has been convicted of Beverly's murder. Her case remains one of Ontario's most enduring cold cases, and her family continues to seek answers nearly fifty years later.
