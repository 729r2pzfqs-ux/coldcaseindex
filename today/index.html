Cases that began on October 29

Conviction 2006

Dana Lynn Dodd (Lavender Doe)

On October 29, 2006, the burning body of an unidentified young woman was found on an oil lease near Kilgore, Texas; she became known as Lavender Doe after the sweater she wore.

Texas Unidentified Person Age 21
Unsolved 2022

Shanquella Robinson

Shanquella Robinson, 25, of Charlotte, North Carolina, died on October 29, 2022, at a rental villa in San José del Cabo, Mexico, during a trip with six companions. A video showed her being struck by a travel companion.

Mexico Homicide Age 25
