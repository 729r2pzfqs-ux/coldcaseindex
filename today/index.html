On This Day: November 18

Cases in the index whose incident or disappearance happened on November 18, in any year, followed by other dated developments recorded on this date: arrests, verdicts, identifications and discoveries. This page is rebuilt daily. See all of November, browse by month, or follow recent updates.

Cases that began on November 18

Conviction 1978

Rep. Leo Ryan & Jonestown Victims

U.S. Congressman Leo Ryan was shot dead at the Port Kaituma airstrip in Guyana on November 18, 1978, along with four others, while investigating Jim Jones's Peoples Temple.

Guyana Multiple Homicide
Conviction 1986

Helle Crafts

Helle Crafts, a 39-year-old Pan Am flight attendant, was murdered by her husband Richard Crafts at their Newtown, Connecticut home in November 1986.

Connecticut Homicide Age 39
Unsolved 1987

Dardeen Family

Keith Dardeen, his pregnant wife Elaine, and their young son were murdered in and near their mobile home outside Ina, Illinois, in one of southern Illinois's most notorious unsolved crimes.

Illinois Multiple Homicide
Conviction 1993

Angie Housman

Nine-year-old Angie Housman was abducted after leaving her school bus in St. Ann, Missouri, on November 18, 1993. A hunter found her body nine days later in the Busch Wildlife Area in St. Charles County.

Missouri Homicide Age 9
Conviction 2012

Dylan Redwine

Thirteen-year-old Dylan Redwine was last seen on November 18, 2012, during a court-ordered visit to his father's home near Vallecito Reservoir in southwest Colorado. His remains were found in 2013 and 2015.

Colorado Homicide Age 13
