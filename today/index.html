Cases that began on November 23

Solved 1972

Terri Lynn Hollis

Eleven-year-old Terri Lynn Hollis disappeared while riding her bicycle in Torrance, California on Thanksgiving Day 1972 and was found dead the next day in Ventura County. She had been sexually assaulted and strangled.

California Homicide Age 11
Solved 2006

Alexander Litvinenko

Former Russian spy Alexander Litvinenko was poisoned with polonium-210 in London. A British inquiry concluded Russian President Putin probably approved the operation. Two suspects were identified.

United Kingdom Homicide Age 43
Conviction 2012

Jordan Davis

Seventeen-year-old Jordan Davis was shot at a Jacksonville gas station by Michael Dunn following an argument over loud music. Dunn fired into the vehicle claiming self-defense.

Florida Homicide Age 17
