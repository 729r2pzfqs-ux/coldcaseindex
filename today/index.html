Cases that began on November 20

Conviction 1901

Nell Cropsey

Nineteen-year-old Ella Maud 'Nell' Cropsey disappeared from her family's home beside the Pasquotank River in Elizabeth City, North Carolina, in November 1901; her body was found in the river on December 27.

Unsolved 2006

The Eastbound Strangler (Atlantic City Four)

Four women, all involved in sex work and struggling with addiction, were found strangled and posed face-down in a drainage ditch behind the Golden Key Motel outside Atlantic City in November 2006.
