Cases that began on November 28

Unsolved 1953

Frank Olson

Army biochemist Frank Olson, 43, died on November 28, 1953, after falling from a window of the Hotel Statler in New York, nine days after CIA colleagues gave him LSD without his knowledge. The death was treated as a suicide.

New York Suspicious Death Age 43
Unsolved 1969

Betsy Aardsma

Betsy Aardsma, a 22-year-old Penn State English graduate student, was stabbed once in the chest in the stacks of the Pattee Library on November 28, 1969, the day after Thanksgiving. No one has ever been charged.

Katrice Lee

Katrice Lee, the daughter of a British soldier, disappeared on her second birthday, November 28, 1981, from a NAAFI shopping complex at Schloss Neuhaus near Paderborn, West Germany.
