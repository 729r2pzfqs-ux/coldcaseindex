Cases that began on November 4

No Conviction 1968

Jane Durrua

Jane Durrua, 13, of Keansburg, New Jersey, disappeared on November 4, 1968, and was found dead in a field in Middletown the next morning.

New Jersey Homicide Age 13
Solved 1989

Grissom Family Murders (Danny Rolling Confession)

William "Tom" Grissom, 55, his daughter Julie, 24, and his grandson Sean, 8, were killed in their Shreveport home on November 4, 1989. Danny Rolling was long suspected but never charged in Louisiana.

Louisiana Serial Killer Victims
Unsolved 2018

Tamla Horsford

Tamla Horsford, a Black mother of five, was found dead at a sleepover party at a white friend's home in Cumming, Georgia. Her death was ruled accidental but her family disputes this.
