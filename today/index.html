Cases that began on October 31

Unsolved 1955

Steven Damman

Two-year-old Steven Damman disappeared on October 31, 1955, after his mother left him and his baby sister in a stroller outside a store in East Meadow, Long Island. The stroller was found nearby with only his sister inside.

New York Missing Person Age 2
Identified 1979

Debra Jackson ("Orange Socks")

A young woman's body was found in a culvert off Interstate 35 near Georgetown, Texas, on Halloween 1979, wearing nothing but a pair of orange socks.

Texas Unidentified Person Age 23
Conviction 2005

Teresa Halbach

Freelance photographer Teresa Halbach disappeared after photographing a vehicle at the Avery Salvage Yard. Her remains were found on the property. Steven Avery and his nephew Brendan Dassey were both convicted of murder.
