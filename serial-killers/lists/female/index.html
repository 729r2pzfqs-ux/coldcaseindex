Female Serial Killers in the United States

Women convicted of, or officially linked to, three or more murders. Poisoning and killings of family members or patients recur in this group far more than in the male profiles.

ColdCaseIndex currently lists 13 serial killers in this group, out of 96 profiles. The list is computed from the dataset on every build, so it changes as profiles are added.
