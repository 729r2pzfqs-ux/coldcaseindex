Serial Killers Who Were Never Caught

Series in which the offender was never identified, or was identified but never taken into custody. The linked case files hold the victims and the investigation.

ColdCaseIndex currently lists 12 serial killers in this group, out of 96 profiles. The list is computed from the dataset on every build, so it changes as profiles are added.

Axeman of New Orleans

The Axeman

Unidentified. An unknown attacker killed at least six people and injured six more in night-time axe attacks on homes in New Orleans and Gretna, Louisiana, between May 1918 and October 1919, most of them Italian-American grocers.

Louisiana At least 6 killed and 6 injured in the attacks attributed to the series in 1918 and 1919; historians dispute which attacks belong to it, and totals of up to 17 killed have been cited suspected Unknown

The Mad Butcher of Kingsbury Run

Unidentified. An unknown offender decapitated and dismembered 12 officially counted victims in Cleveland, Ohio, between 1935 and 1938, most found in or near Kingsbury Run.

Ohio 12 officially counted (7 men, 5 women); some researchers suggest 20 or more suspected Unknown

Phantom Killer of Texarkana

The Phantom Killer

Unidentified. An attacker shot and beat eight people in four attacks around Texarkana, Texas and Arkansas, between February and May 1946, killing five.

Texas 5 killed and 3 wounded in 4 attacks suspected Unknown
Unidentified 1968 to 1969

Zodiac Killer

Zodiac

Unidentified. The Zodiac Killer attacked seven people in Northern California between December 1968 and October 1969, killing five, and taunted police and newspapers with letters and ciphers.

California 5 confirmed killed and 2 survivors; 37 claimed in a 1974 letter suspected At large

Freeway Phantom

The Freeway Phantom

Unidentified. The Freeway Phantom abducted and strangled six Black girls and young women, aged 10 to 18, in Washington, D.C., between April 1971 and September 1972, leaving their bodies beside roads in D.C.

The Babysitter Killer

Unidentified. Four children aged 10 to 12 were abducted in Oakland County, Michigan, between February 1976 and March 1977 and found dead days or weeks later.

Connecticut River Valley Killer

The Valley Killer

Unidentified. At least seven women were stabbed to death in the Connecticut River Valley of New Hampshire and Vermont between 1978 and 1987, and a pregnant woman survived a 1988 attack.

New Hampshire At least 7 women killed; 1 woman attacked and survived suspected At large

I-70 Killer

I-70 Serial Killer

Unidentified. Six shop workers were shot in the head in small stores in Indiana, Kansas and Missouri between April 8 and May 7, 1992, all linked by a .22-caliber weapon.
