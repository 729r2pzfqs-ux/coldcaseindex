California Serial Killers

ColdCaseIndex documents 3 serial killers whose murders took place in California, with first known killings from 1972 to 1975. 3 of them were caught; 1 linked case file document their victims. The earliest series is Edmund Kemper (1972) and the most recent is Joseph James DeAngelo (1975). Profiles are added in order of public interest, so this page is not a complete census of the state.

Convicted 1972 to 1973

Edmund Kemper

The Co-ed Killer

Imprisoned. Edmund Kemper was convicted by a Santa Cruz County jury in 1973 of eight counts of first-degree murder for killing six young women, his mother and her friend in 1972 and 1973.

Convicted 1975 to 1986

Joseph James DeAngelo

The Golden State Killer

Imprisoned for life. Joseph DeAngelo, the Golden State Killer, pleaded guilty in 2020 to 13 California murders committed from 1975 to 1986 and admitted dozens of rapes; he was identified in 2018 through forensic genetic genealogy.
