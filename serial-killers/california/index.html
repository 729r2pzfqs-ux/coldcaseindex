California Serial Killers

ColdCaseIndex documents 20 serial killers whose murders took place in California, with first known killings from 1968 to 1990. 19 of them were caught; 11 linked case files document their victims. The earliest series is Zodiac Killer (1968) and the most recent is Keith Hunter Jesperson (1990). Profiles are added in order of public interest, so this page is not a complete census of the state.
