Case files

The victims of these offenders are documented as cases, with their own timelines and sources. 89 serial murder case files are indexed under serial killer cases; each profile links to the case files it is connected to.

How these profiles are compiled

Each profile is written from court opinions, corrections records, official releases and established reporting, with every claim attributed in the text and every source listed and checked on the date shown. Nobody is described as a killer without a conviction, a formal charge or an official attribution, and pending cases say "charged", not "guilty". Victims are named only where the public record names them.

Corrections, including requests from victims' families: info@coldcaseindex.com.
