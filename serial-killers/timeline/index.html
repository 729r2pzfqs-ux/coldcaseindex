Serial Killers Timeline, by Decade

Every profile in the dataset placed by the year of the first known murder. Decades with more profiles reflect both the history of serial murder in the United States, which peaked in the 1970s and 1980s, and which profiles have been written so far.

1890s: 1 profile

  1. 1891H. H. Holmes (Herman Webster Mudgett) (Henry Howard Holmes), Illinois, Pennsylvania, Indiana, active 1891 to 1894, 1 convicted, executed.

1900s: 1 profile

  1. 1900Belle Gunness (Brynhild Paulsdatter Størseth), Indiana, Illinois, active 1900 to 1908, 14 to more than 40 (remains of at least 11 people dug up on the farm, plus three children and a headless woman in the fire ruins) suspected, unknown.

1910s: 1 profile

  1. 1918Axeman of New Orleans (The Axeman), Louisiana, active 1918 to 1919, At least 6 killed and 6 injured in the attacks attributed to the series in 1918 and 1919; historians dispute which attacks belong to it, and totals of up to 17 killed have been cited suspected, unknown.
