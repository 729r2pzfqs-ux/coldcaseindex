Method and caveats

  • Counts use the legal status, fate, states and dates recorded on each profile; see the schema in the site source.
  • Years to arrest is the arrest year minus the year of the first known murder; profiles with no arrest are left out.
  • A profile is counted under every state in which it records murders, so state totals can exceed the number of profiles.
  • Murder convictions sum the convicted counts only; confessed and suspected numbers are not added.

Figures from this site are recomputed from the published profile dataset on every build. The dataset grows in order of public interest and is not a census of serial murder; the national figures quoted above come from the sources named next to them. To report an error, email info@coldcaseindex.com.
