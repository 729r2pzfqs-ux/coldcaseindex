Washington Serial Killers

ColdCaseIndex documents 6 serial killers whose murders took place in Washington, with first known killings from 1974 to 1991. 6 of them were caught; 5 linked case files document their victims. The earliest series is Ted Bundy (1974) and the most recent is Shelly Knotek (1991). Profiles are added in order of public interest, so this page is not a complete census of the state.

Convicted 1974 to 1978

Ted Bundy

Theodore Robert Bundy

Executed in Florida in 1989. Ted Bundy was convicted of three murders in Florida and confessed to 30 killings of young women across Washington, Oregon, Utah, Colorado, Idaho and Florida between 1974 and 1978.

Kenneth Bianchi

The Hillside Strangler

Imprisoned for life. Kenneth Bianchi, one of the two Hillside Stranglers, pleaded guilty in 1979 to five Los Angeles murders and two Bellingham, Washington murders; his cousin Angelo Buono was convicted of nine killings in 1983.

Convicted 1982 to 1998

Gary Ridgway

The Green River Killer

Imprisoned. Gary Ridgway, the Green River Killer, pleaded guilty in 2003 to 48 murders of women and girls in King County, Washington, between 1982 and 1998, and to a 49th in 2011.

Convicted 1990 to 1995

Keith Hunter Jesperson

The Happy Face Killer

Serving life. Long-haul trucker Keith Hunter Jesperson, the Happy Face Killer, is held at the Oregon State Penitentiary for murders of women committed between 1990 and 1995; eight killings in six states are considered confirmed.

Green River Killer Victims

Gary Ridgway, the Green River Killer, murdered at least 49 women in Washington State. He targeted mostly sex workers and runaways. Ridgway was convicted in 2003 after DNA evidence linked him to four victims.
